Section 2 - Free Camera

Checkpoint: Free Camera

Trigger: Reset to the main Orc base, centered on the Fortress.

Narration: {Heading: FREE CAMERA}
Narration: There are four ways to freely move the camera.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: Firstly, you can use "edge pan" by {Input: moving the mouse} to the edges of the screen.
Narration: Edge pan is the most common way to move the camera. It is quick and intuitive.
Narration: The drawback is that your mouse cursor will have to leave the center of the screen, where the action happens. Moving it back may cost you some time.
Narration: Try using edge pan!
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: The second way to freely move the camera is to {Input: press and hold the Middle Mouse Button} and drag the screen.
Narration: This is slower, but it allows you to make small adjustments quickly, without having to move the cursor away from the action.
Narration: Try dragging the camera!
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: The third way to move the camera is using the minimap. It is displayed on the bottom left of the screen.
Narration: You can {Input: click on the map} to center the camera on that location, or {Input: click and drag across the map} to move it.
Narration: Try moving the camera using the minimap!
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: The last way to freely move the camera is using the keyboard. The default keys for this are the {Input: arrow keys}.
Narration: These are a bit out of the way for both your left and your right hand, but some people prefer moving the camera using their keyboard hand.
Narration: Try moving the camera using the arrow keys!
Progression: Wait for Continue.


Section 3 - Zooming

Checkpoint: Zooming

Narration: {Heading: ZOOMING}
Narration: The default camera distance is 1650 units. This can be changed in the settings.
Narration: Click the Quest button on the top left, or press {Input: F9}, for more details.
Trigger: Reveal the optional quest "Changing Default Camera Distance", play the quest-discovery sound, and highlight the Quest button.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: You can change the camera distance while in-game. The default control for this is rolling the {Input: Mouse Wheel}.
Narration: A closer camera makes controlling units easier, while having the camera further back can help to keep a clear view of a situation.
Narration: It is best to set zoom to a default value that allows you to do both. You can always zoom in or out during play.
Narration: Feel free to set the default value to something that feels right for you!
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: After zooming in or out using the {Input: Mouse Wheel}, you can reset the current camera distance back to the default value you chose.
Narration: The default key to do this is {Input: F5}.
Narration: Change the camera zoom, and then reset it!
Progression: Wait for Continue.


Section 4 - Base Camera

Checkpoint: Base Camera

Trigger: Reset the camera to center on the main Orc base's Fortress.

Narration: {Heading: BASE CAMERA}
Narration: Now let us take a look at the camera commands you can use for your bases and units.
Narration: In this tutorial, you are in control of three different bases.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Trigger: Center the camera on the Great Hall building of the tertiary Orc base.
Narration: Each of your bases has a main building at a different tier.
Narration: For Orcs the tier 1 building is called a Great Hall.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Trigger: Center the camera on the Stronghold building of the secondary Orc base.
Narration: The tier 2 building is called a Stronghold.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Trigger: Center the camera on the Fortress building of the main Orc base.
Narration: And the tier 3 building is called a Fortress.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: {Heading: JUMP TO BASE}
Narration: You can return to your main buildings, and cycle between them.
Narration: The default key for this is {Input: Backspace}.
Narration: When doing this, the first main building the camera will jump to is the one with the highest tier, the next will be of the next highest tier.
Narration: If several have the same tier, the camera will jump to the oldest ones first.
Narration: Try cycling through your main buildings!
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Trigger: Begin the accelerated unit, building and research completion demonstrations.
Narration: {Heading: NOTIFICATIONS}
Narration: Throughout the game, you will receive notifications, with associated audio callouts.
Narration: Each of the notifications has a location. You can jump to the most recent location, and then cycle through the 8 most recent ones.
Narration: The default key for this is {Input: Space}.
Narration: These notifications can be for finished units, buildings, or researches.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Trigger: Begin the attacks against the main and tertiary bases.
Narration: You also get notified when you are being attacked.
Narration: Once you are being attacked, pressing {Input: Space} is the quickest way to get to the action.
Narration: This is easier than first checking on the minimap and manually clicking where the enemy units are shown to be.
Narration: Try cycling through your notifications!
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: {Heading: IDLE WORKERS}
Narration: You can automatically center the camera on idle worker units, and cycle through them.
Narration: The default key for this is {Input: Tilde}.
Narration: Its label varies on your keyboard layout. It is between the Tabulator and the Escape keys.
Narration: Try cycling through your workers!
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: If you want to be able to just select idle workers without automatically centering the camera on them, you can set it to require a {Input: double-tap}.
Narration: Click the Quest button on the top left, or press {Input: F9}, for more details.
Trigger: Reveal the optional quest "Idle Worker Double-Tap", play the quest-discovery sound, and highlight the Quest button.
Progression: Wait for Continue.


Section 5 - Army Camera

Checkpoint: Army Camera

Trigger: Center the camera on the main Orc army.

Narration: {Heading: ARMY CAMERA}
Narration: There are two main ways of navigating the camera to your army units.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: By pressing {Input: F1}, {Input: F2} or {Input: F3}, you can select your hero units.
Narration: {Input: Double-tapping} the key centers the camera on the hero.
Narration: Which hero is bound to which key depends on the order you trained them in the current game, with your first hero always being bound to {Input: F1} and so on.
Narration: Alternatively, you can {Input: click the hero icons} on the top left of the screen.
Narration: These keys cannot be changed in-game.
Narration: Try moving the camera to your heroes!
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: If you want to freely assign units or buildings to keys, you have to use {Term: Control Groups}.
Narration: {Input: Click} one of your units, {Input: hold CTRL}, and then {Input: press 1} on your keyboard!
Narration: {Input: (CTRL+1)}
Narration: This assigns them to Control Group 1.
Narration: You can create a control group for every number key.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: Control groups can be used to control and divide your army according to type and function.
Narration: How to best use control groups will be covered in more detail in a later tutorial.
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: After forming it, {Input: pressing 1} will select the units of Control Group 1.
Narration: {Input: Double-tapping} the key moves the camera to the location of the group's units.
Narration: Try to assign and use different control groups!
Progression: Wait for Continue.

Narration: Instead of locking the camera, you can also center it on the unit by {Input: clicking its portrait} at the bottom of the screen, or follow the unit by {Input: clicking and holding} the portrait.
Narration: Try using these commands!
Progression: Wait for Continue.
