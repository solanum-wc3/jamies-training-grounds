# Development review — 13 October 2026

Reviewed the current development changes around Hotkey Trainer, Reflex Training, shared menu/toolbar/end-card presentation, sounds, quest hints, generated-unit cleanup and tutorial navigation. The working tree also contains earlier changes; this is not a claim that every uncommitted file was introduced today.

## Findings addressed

- **Camera Back bypassed entry invalidation.** Its manual narration restoration did not advance the shared tutorial generation or clear announced headings. Back now begins a new entry. The navigation fixture covers rapid revisits and ensures an old callback cannot become valid again. Idle-worker preparation remains immediate and scoped to its two related narration steps; the tutorial does not wait for training completion to make idle workers available.
- **Reflex native casts lacked reliable prerequisites.** A zero-mana Peasant carried spells that normally require caster training. Real caster types now receive mana, the actual research and hero skills. All 21 ability/caster/buff associations were checked against installed melee data. Single-race tests apply at least one genuine buff per race. Live confirmation of Warcraft's cast behavior is still needed.
- **Identification particle cleanup was incomplete visually.** Destroying BanishTarget alone allows its native death particles to continue. Each scoped mark now becomes invisible and zero-scale before destruction. Stage tests verify this cleanup before the next list is shown.
- **Effect labels could announce a pending spell.** Labels wait for the corresponding native buff. Supported effects on bystanders are cleared between trials, and an expired effect clears its label immediately.
- **Hotkey input could depend on selection surviving a cast.** Current-context spell events can confirm the answer after selection changes. Channel, cast and effect phases all match the exact source/ability. Tests reject a different ability and cover Cleansing Fire on all three phases. Native command-card buttons remain intact; the three-mistake cover affects presentation only.
- **Sound feedback needed distinct lifetimes.** End-card sound state belongs to the session, not disposable frames, preventing replay on redraw/rebuild. Next/retry reset it. Gameplay feedback does not stop the preceding menu/finish-line cue. End-card buttons retain menu sounds, separate from toolbar sounds.

The 33 Lua fixtures pass, including controller-style starts for all five Hotkey pools; fast/delayed Build and Ability Choice input; active native selection; 15 Reflex race combinations; exact 62/50 population budgets; ten growing identification stages; reserved targets and cumulative scoring; pause/menu/load restoration; marker and native-buff cleanup; sound dispatch; quest-hint timing; generated-unit cleanup; tutorial checkpoint restoration and stale-callback rejection. ## Design assessment

Reflex currently combines visual search, spell recognition and clicking speed, so an average time does not tell the player which skill was difficult. The large starting crowd and varied spell list also provide little onboarding or rhythmic payoff. Separate a simple reaction challenge from battlefield-reading tasks before adding more complexity. A small timed prototype with immediate feedback and a clean-answer streak is the next useful experiment; contextual micro decisions can follow after that loop is enjoyable.
