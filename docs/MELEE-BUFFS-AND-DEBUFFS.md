# Melee buffs and debuffs

Scope: the four melee races, including their heroes. Items, orb items and
neutral heroes are excluded. This is a gameplay classification, not a claim
that every effect has a dispellable buff icon. Values and durations are omitted
because balance patches change them. Names/effects were checked against the
installed melee catalogue and Blizzard's unit reference.

## Human

Buffs / beneficial temporary effects:
- Inner Fire — Priest: damage and armor bonus.
- Invisibility — Sorceress: invisibility until broken.
- Divine Shield — Paladin: self invulnerability.
- Avatar — Mountain King: temporary combat bonuses and spell immunity.

Debuffs / disabling effects:
- Slow — Sorceress: movement and attack-speed reduction.
- Polymorph — Sorceress: disables an enemy through transformation.
- Banish — Blood Mage: ethereal state and movement slow; can also be cast on allies.
- Storm Bolt — Mountain King: stun.
- Thunder Clap — Mountain King: movement and attack-speed reduction.
- Aerial Shackles — Dragonhawk Rider: immobilizes/disables an air target while channeling.
- Cloud — Dragonhawk Rider: disables ranged building attacks in the area.

Passive auras: Devotion Aura (Paladin), Brilliance Aura (Archmage).
Attack-triggered debuff: Bash (Mountain King), applying stun.
Other self state: Defend (Footman), trading movement speed for protection.

References: [Priest](https://classic.battle.net/war3/human/units/priest.shtml),
[Sorceress](https://classic.battle.net/war3/human/units/sorceress.shtml),
[Mountain King](https://classic.battle.net/war3/human/units/mountainking.shtml),
[Dragonhawk Rider](https://classic.battle.net/war3/human/units/dragonhawkrider.shtml).

## Orc

Buffs / beneficial temporary effects:
- Bloodlust — Shaman: movement and attack-speed bonus.
- Lightning Shield — Shaman: persistent damage around the affected unit, including friendly fire.
- Spirit Link — Spirit Walker: shares incoming damage among linked units.
- Healing Ward — Witch Doctor: regeneration in the ward's area.
- Wind Walk — Blademaster: invisibility, movement bonus and passage through units.
- Bladestorm — Blademaster: channeled damage and spell immunity.
- Big Bad Voodoo — Shadow Hunter: invulnerability for nearby friendly units, excluding himself.
- Berserk — Troll Berserker: faster attacks with increased damage taken.

Debuffs / disabling effects:
- Purge — Shaman: slowing effect as well as buff removal.
- Hex — Shadow Hunter: disables the target through transformation.
- War Stomp — Tauren Chieftain: stun.
- Earthquake — Far Seer: movement slow in its area.
- Ensnare — Raider: immobilizes the target.
- Stasis Trap — Witch Doctor: area stun when the ward triggers.

Passive auras: Endurance Aura (Tauren Chieftain), War Drums (Kodo Beast).
Attack-triggered debuff: Envenomed Spears (Wind Rider), applying poison damage.
Devour removes a unit from play; it is a separate containment mechanic rather
than an ordinary removable debuff.

References: [Shaman](https://classic.battle.net/war3/orc/units/shaman.shtml),
[Spirit Walker](https://classic.battle.net/war3/orc/units/spiritwalker.shtml),
[Shadow Hunter](https://classic.battle.net/war3/orc/units/shadowhunter.shtml),
[Witch Doctor](https://classic.battle.net/war3/orc/units/witchdoctor.shtml).

## Night Elf

Buffs / beneficial temporary effects:
- Roar — Druid of the Claw: attack-damage bonus.
- Rejuvenation — Druid of the Claw: healing over time.
- Tranquility — Keeper of the Grove: healing in the channelled area.
- Mana Flare — Faerie Dragon: channelled spell-punishment effect and self armor bonus.
- Metamorphosis — Demon Hunter: temporary enhanced form; a transformation rather than an ordinary dispellable buff.

Debuffs / disabling effects:
- Entangling Roots — Keeper of the Grove: immobilization and damage over time.
- Faerie Fire — Druid of the Talon: armor reduction and vision of the target.
- Cyclone — Druid of the Talon: temporarily removes the target from combat.
- Shadow Strike — Warden: movement slow and damage over time.

Passive auras: Trueshot Aura (Priestess of the Moon), Thorns Aura (Keeper of the Grove).
Attack-triggered debuff: Slow Poison (Dryad), applying poison and slowing effects.
Other states/commands: Shadowmeld, Phase Shift, Bear Form, Storm Crow Form,
War Club and Taunt. These should not be conflated with ordinary dispellable
caster buffs/debuffs. Eat Tree is channelled self healing.

References: [Druid of the Claw](https://classic.battle.net/war3/nightelf/units/druidoftheclaw.shtml),
[Druid of the Talon](https://classic.battle.net/war3/nightelf/units/druidofthetalon.shtml),
[Keeper of the Grove](https://classic.battle.net/war3/nightelf/units/keepergrove.shtml),
[Warden](https://classic.battle.net/war3/nightelf/units/warden.shtml).

## Undead

Buffs / beneficial temporary effects:
- Frost Armor — Lich: armor bonus; also slows melee attackers.
- Anti-magic Shell — Banshee: protection against magical damage.
- Unholy Frenzy — Necromancer: attack-speed bonus with damage over time to its recipient.

Debuffs / disabling effects:
- Curse — Banshee: chance for the target's attacks to miss.
- Cripple — Necromancer: movement, attack-speed and damage reduction.
- Sleep — Dreadlord: sleep disable.
- Frost Nova — Lich: movement and attack-speed reduction alongside damage.
- Frost Armor retaliation — Lich's protected unit: slow on melee attackers.
- Impale — Crypt Lord: airborne disable followed by stun.
- Inferno impact — Dreadlord: area stun.
- Web — Crypt Fiend: brings an air unit to the ground and immobilizes it.

Passive auras: Unholy Aura (Death Knight), Vampiric Aura (Dreadlord).
Other applied effects: Disease Cloud (Abomination/Meat Wagon), frost attack
slow (Frost Wyrm/Nerubian Tower), and building disable from Freezing Breath
(Frost Wyrm). Possession includes a channelled disable before permanent
ownership transfer. Burrow and Stone Form are self states; Cannibalize is
channelled self healing.

References: [Banshee](https://classic.battle.net/war3/undead/units/banshee.shtml),
[Necromancer](https://classic.battle.net/war3/undead/units/necromancer.shtml),
[Lich](https://classic.battle.net/war3/undead/units/lich.shtml),
[Dreadlord](https://classic.battle.net/war3/undead/units/dreadlord.shtml).

## Buff removal / transfer

These interact with buffs rather than simply applying a new positive effect:
- Human: Dispel Magic (Priest), Spell Steal (Spell Breaker).
- Orc: Purge (Shaman), Disenchant (Spirit Walker), Mirror Image self-cleanse (Blademaster).
- Night Elf: Abolish Magic (Dryad), Detonate (Wisp).
- Undead: Devour Magic (Destroyer).

Not all roots, stuns, aura effects or transformations share dispel rules.
Any future exercise should use a separate per-effect dispel classification.
