# Decision 0002: Preserve Twisted Meadows around a reusable practice district

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-23

## Context

The successor map needs ordinary Warcraft III terrain for realistic tutorials and open spaces for mechanical exercises. Replacing a large portion of Twisted Meadows immediately would make it harder to judge scale and would discard useful terrain before the tutorial layout is settled.

## Decision

Keep Twisted Meadows unchanged outside a northeastern work area. Inside that area, remove placed units and terrain objects, flatten the playable surface, use varied Lordaeron Summer grass and earth textures, and surround the district with raised impassable terrain.

The foundation build contains two connected practice spaces. Their final subdivisions and decoration will be decided as exercises are added.

## Consequences

- Realistic terrain remains available for the camera tutorial and later match-like lessons.
- Early mechanical exercises have a reusable open district.
- The district can be expanded or reshaped without rebuilding the whole map.
- Build validation must prove that terrain, pathing, units, and objects outside the district remain unchanged.
