============================================================
Main briefing
============================================================

Sometimes a valid strategy is simply to stop your opponent from doing what they are doing by standing in the way. Be it to get more hits in on them, or to stop them from effectively doing anything.

This exercise trains you in blocking the movement of other units. You control a Footman, and using the Move, Stop and Hold Position commands, try to slow down a series of units for as long as possible.

The usual {Button: Training} has you choose between the race of the units you want to practice against, and you will encounter a series of that race's units.
Alternatively, you can freely customize which unit you want to practice with.
