Main briefing
============================================================

Orcs have a unique way to free a unit from being surrounded.
During Blademaster's {Term: Windwalk}, he has no unit collision.
This allows him to "pop out" a surrounded unit, after getting collision again.

In these trainings, you can practice freeing units from surrounds like this,
or you can learn the counterplay against a Windwalk Pop.

Check the {Button: Quest Log} for precise instructions!
