Main briefing
============================================================

Since Gargoyles are flying units, they do not obstruct the movement of ground units. Their {Button: Stone Form} changes this: once they land as statues, they occupy space like ordinary ground units.

In this, you can practice surrounding units using Stone Form.
There is a {Term: Setup} for every ability that can help in a surround, which Undead can use, and some just for units.
To select a specific unit setup, use the {Input: Setup button}.

Check the {Button: Quest Log} for precise instructions on how to surround using Gargoyles!
