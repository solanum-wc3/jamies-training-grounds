Main briefing
============================================================

Sometimes the best thing to do in a fight, is to run away, since any unit that survives the battle can be healed and fight again.
Units have to stand still for a moment to do an attack, so they are much less effective when having to chase an enemy.

By blocking a unit on all four sides, you can box them in and stop their movement completely.
This can allow you to freely finish off enemy units, especially important ones like heroes.

In this exercise you can practice surrounding units, by {Yellow Font: using your own units and their abilities}.
You will go through two stages for every slow ability of your race, and one or two just using your units.
