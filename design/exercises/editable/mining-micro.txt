============================================================
Main briefing
============================================================

When mining with less than 5 workers, the game slows down the movement speed of Human and Orc gold workers, to normalize gold income.

By manually giving mining orders, you can temporarily bypass that slowdown.

With four workers - especially on short, diagonal mine positions - this can recover much of the income you'd normally need five workers for, freeing up a worker to build, or to harvest lumber, which can give you an edge in the early game.

Here, you can train this micro skill.
Check the {Button: Quest Log} for explanations on how to do it!
