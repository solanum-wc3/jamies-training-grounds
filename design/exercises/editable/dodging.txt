avoiding
============================================================

In this training there are two categories of things to avoid.
The first being abilities with an {Term: Area} effect, meaning they do not target a unit.
They either target the ground, or a direction, or automatically affect an area around the unit that uses them.

The other category is a specialized training to avoid the {Term: Devour} ability of the Orc's {Gold Font: Kodo Beast}.

Choose what you want to train against!

============================================================
areaIntro
============================================================

To avoid an area ability, you have to move outside of the affected area, either through {Term: Movement}, or through the use of your own {Term: Ability}.

In this training, the enemy will repeatedly try to hit your unit with its ability. You will succeed if you avoid it for 15 seconds.
Depending on the ability, this can be very hard, or even impossible, so pay attention to telegraphing behavior through targeting and wind-up animations!

Your notes:


============================================================
disjoining
============================================================

To disjoin a projectile, you need to make your unit stop being a valid target after the attacker has thrown it, but before it has hit your unit.
If you do this before the attacker has thrown it, you have avoided the effects, but it has cost your enemy nothing.

Choose which kinds of projectiles you want to disjoin!

============================================================
root
============================================================

It is possible to dodge and evade many attacks and abilities.

When it takes the form of a missile that flies at your unit, it is possible to {Term: Disjoin} that missile, meaning to make it miss its target, effectively dodging it.
When the ability merely has a range or an area of effect, it is possible to {Term: Avoid} the ability.

Both of these techniques have two advantages: You are making your enemy waste resources (Time, Cooldowns or Mana), and you are preventing your own units from being damaged or disabled.

Choose which kind of dodging you want to train!
